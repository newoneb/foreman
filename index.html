The ownership transition

A generation of private companies is changing hands.

Most will close rather than sell. Owners who prepare early have more options when the time comes.

6 million

small businesses are expected to change hands in the United States by 2035.

McKinsey, via Fortune

92%

of small and midsize business exits in 2022 were closures. Five percent were sales.

McKinsey Institute for Economic Mobility, via Fortune

1 in 3

Fewer than one in three owners has an exit plan. Fewer than one in ten can name their company’s value within 10%.

How we work

Four commitments on every engagement.

  • Independent

    We charge no fees and take no commissions or referral payments, so our only interest is an accurate answer.

  • Confidential

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement before any figures are shared, and the owner decides who sees our work.

  • Reviewed

    Every analysis is checked by a second team before it reaches an owner.

  • Early

    We prefer to work with owners years before a transaction, while there is still time to act on what we find.
