What we do

Two practices for the two questions every owner faces.

Operations improvement

Not planning to sell? Make it run better, with less of you.

  • Pricing and margin by customer and service line
  • Working capital and the cash cycle
  • Owner dependence and documented processes
  • Monthly reporting the owner can rely on
See operations improvement

Succession advisory

Planning a sale or handover? Know what it is worth and who can take it on.

  • Normalized earnings and an indicative value range
  • A financing test against current SBA lending standards
  • Family, managers or an outside buyer, compared
  • A preparation plan with priorities and timing
See succession advisory

How an engagement works

About eight weeks, and a few hours of your time.

12345 of 5
  1. 1

    Introductory conversation

    We learn about the company and what you want to understand. If we are a good fit, we propose a scope.

    About 30 minutes

  2. 2

    Agreement and scope

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement and agree in writing on the questions we will answer and what you will receive.

    One meeting

  3. 3

    Records and interviews

    You share records through your private client portal. We interview you and, with your permission, key managers.

    Two or three conversations

  4. 4

    Analysis and recheck

    Your team builds the analysis, and its senior members recheck every figure before anything reaches you.

    Weekly updates in your portal

  5. 5

    Final presentation

    We present our findings and recommendations in person. The report is yours to keep and to share with your advisors.

    About 90 minutes

Who we work with

Owner-operated companies across Chicago and its suburbs.

  • Commercial and home services
  • Specialty trades
  • Distribution
  • Light manufacturing
  • Business services
  • Established local businesses

Usually led by one or two owners, with at least two years of tax returns or financial statements. Some are planning a transition; many only want the company to run better.

An outside team with no stake in the outcome.

No fees
Our members learn by doing real work. Owners receive it without charge, and we accept no commissions or referral payments.
Confidential
A non-disclosure agreement comes before any records. Only your team and its reviewers see your figures.
Rigorous
Analysts are trained before they join an engagement, and each team’s senior members recheck every deliverable.
Practical
We focus on the few decisions and changes that matter most to your company, and we show our reasoning.

For students

Learn how companies work by advising the people who built them.

Teams of three to five analysts work directly with an owner, from the first interview to the final presentation. Training comes first, so no finance background is needed.
