We help owners build companies that are worth more and depend less on them.

Every owner-led company eventually faces two questions. Who will run it next, and is it ready for them? Our two practices answer both.

Succession advisory

For owners planning a sale or a handover in the next few years. We show what the business is worth, what a buyer or lender will question, and how to prepare.

  • Normalized earnings and an indicative value range
  • A financing test against current SBA lending standards
  • Family, management or outside buyer, compared on your terms
  • A preparation plan with priorities and timing
Succession advisory

Operations improvement

For owners who want a stronger, more profitable company that can run without them. We find where margin and cash are lost and what to change first.

  • Pricing and margin by customer and service line
  • Working capital and the cash cycle
  • Owner dependence, delegation and documented processes
  • Monthly reporting the owner can rely on

Succession advisory

A readiness review before you speak with buyers.

  1. We restate your earnings the way a buyer and a lender will, and test every adjustment.
  2. We estimate a value range and check whether a typical buyer could finance it.
  3. We list what would lower the price, and what to fix first.

Succession advisory

A clear comparison of the paths to a new owner.

  1. We compare transfer to family, sale to managers and sale to an outside buyer on price, timing and your income.
  2. We assess whether the next team is ready to run the company, and what they still need.
  3. We set out a transition plan your attorney and CPA can build on.

Operations improvement

A margin and cash review that ends in a short list of changes.

  1. We measure profitability by customer, job type or service line.
  2. We trace where cash is tied up in receivables, inventory and payables.
  3. We recommend the few changes with the largest effect, in order.

Operations improvement

A plan to make the company less dependent on you.

  1. We map the customers, decisions and knowledge that sit with you alone.
  2. We help document key processes and decide who owns each one.
  3. We set up simple monthly reporting so you can step back with confidence.

How an engagement works

Most engagements run for about eight weeks. Your own time commitment is a few hours in total.

What working with us looks like
12345 of 5

    Introductory conversation

    We learn about the company and what you want to understand. If we are a good fit, we propose a scope.

    About 30 minutes

  2. 2

    Agreement and scope

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement and agree in writing on the questions we will answer and what you will receive.

    One meeting

  3. 3

    Records and interviews

    You share records through your private client portal. We interview you and, with your permission, key managers.

    Two or three conversations

  4. 4

    Analysis and review

    Your team builds the analysis. A second team reviews every figure before anything reaches you.

    Weekly updates in your portal

  5. 5

    Final presentation

    We present our findings and recommendations in person. The report is yours to keep and to share with your advisors.

    About 90 minutes

Why owners work with us

An outside team, a careful process and no commercial interest in the outcome.

  • No fees

    Our members learn by doing real work. Owners receive it without charge, and we accept no commissions or referral payments.

  • Confidential

    A non-disclosure agreement comes before any records. Only your team and its reviewers see your figures.

  • Rigorous

    Analysts are trained before they join an engagement, and a second team reviews every deliverable.

  • Practical

    We focus on the few decisions and changes that matter most to your company, and we show our reasoning.

Who we work with

Owner-operated companies in Chicago and the surrounding suburbs, usually led by one or two owners.

  • Commercial and home services
  • Specialty trades
  • Distribution
  • Light manufacturing
  • Business services
  • Established local businesses
