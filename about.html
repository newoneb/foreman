A student consulting group, built at the University of Chicago.

Foreman advises the owners of Chicago’s private companies on succession and operations, and trains its members through real work held to a professional standard.

Our purpose

Chicago’s economy rests on thousands of companies built by one or two owners. Many will change hands in the next decade, and most of their owners have never had an outside team look closely at the business.

Foreman gives them that team, without charge. In return, our members gain an education no classroom can offer: what it takes to run a real company, and to help the person who built it decide what comes next.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Independence

    We charge no fees and accept no commissions or referral payments. Our only interest is an accurate answer.

  • Confidentiality

    A non-disclosure agreement precedes any exchange of figures, and owners decide who sees our work.

  • Rigor

    Every analysis is reviewed by a second team before delivery, and we show our assumptions so they can be tested.

  • Restraint

    We stay within our competence. Appraisals, brokerage, tax and legal advice belong to licensed professionals.

How we are organized

Most members work on engagement teams. A small leadership group runs everything that supports them.

  • Engagement teams

    Three to five analysts and an engagement manager, assigned to one client at a time.

  • Review

    Experienced members who check every deliverable before it reaches an owner.

  • Training

    The curriculum and practice cases every new member completes.

  • Client development

    Relationships with owners and with the CPAs, lenders and counselors who refer them.

Affiliation

Foreman is a student organization at the University of Chicago. It does not speak for the University, and its work does not represent the views of the University or its faculty.

Foreman is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, business or real estate broker, appraiser, law firm or accounting firm.
