The analyst experience

You will not need a finance background. You will need curiosity about how a plumbing contractor or a distributor actually makes money.

  • Training first

    New members learn to read financial statements, normalize earnings and analyze operations, and work practice cases before joining a client.

  • Real engagements

    Teams of three to five work directly with an owner, from the first interview to the final presentation.

  • Careful review

    A second team checks every deliverable, so you get detailed feedback on your work each time.

  • A serious community

    Members from every major who want to understand business by doing it, not only by studying it.

What you will learn

The skills behind the advice owners need most.

  • Reading the records

    Business tax returns, profit and loss statements, balance sheets and payroll reports.

  • Normalizing earnings

    Separating one-time items and owner expenses from the real economics of a company.

  • Valuation and lending

    Value ranges from comparable transactions, and the lending standards a buyer must meet.

  • Operations analysis

    Pricing, margin, working capital and the processes behind them.

  • Succession planning

    How companies pass to family, managers or outside buyers, and what each path requires.

  • Working with owners

    How to ask good questions and present difficult findings with care.

Questions students ask

When can I apply?

During each recruiting cycle, a few weeks at the start of a quarter. The dates are on the recruiting calendar above. If applications are closed, leave your email at the bottom of this page and we will tell you when the next cycle opens.

Do I need experience in finance?

No. Training covers what you need. We look for curiosity, care and clear thinking.

Which students can apply?

Any University of Chicago student, in any year and any major.

Are info sessions and coffee chats required?

No. They are the best way to understand the work and meet the people you would work with, and they never count against anyone who couldn't come.

How much time does it take?

A few hours a week while you are on an engagement, through the final presentation.

How do I check my application?

Sign in to the portal with the account you applied with. Your status updates as your application moves forward.

Can I apply again?

Yes, in a later cycle. Many strong members apply more than once.
